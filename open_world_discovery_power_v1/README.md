# Open-World Discovery Power v1

## Core

**Open-world discovery power is the verified capacity to acquire distinct valid objects, estimate residual unseen mass, select the next admissible source by marginal unique value, detect change, and terminate only with an exact, estimated, blocked, or impossible certificate.**

Finding many rows is not power when they are duplicates, invalid, unverifiable, or drawn from one source whose denominator is unknown.

## Scientific synthesis

The slice combines:

- sample coverage and Good–Turing missing mass;
- Chao lower-bound estimation from frequency-of-frequencies;
- two-list capture–recapture via the Chapman estimator;
- adaptive source selection by expected unique gain per cost;
- Logic Power indistinguishability certificates;
- LP-KAL evidence, authority, and honest terminal states.

## Real IAIP diagnostic

Current Notion evidence records:

- 2,574,240 inventory rows;
- 2,571,210 document URLs;
- 2,530,927 physical files;
- 3,030 non-document links;
- 40,283 document URLs without a local physical file.

These figures establish a known acquisition gap. They do not identify the number of documents absent from the inventory itself; that larger unseen universe requires frequency-of-frequencies and independent capture-overlap data.

## Score boundary

- verified baseline: **896/1000**;
- eligible after independent public reconstruction PASS: **918/1000**.

The score remains blocked by continuous/open-ended discovery, dependence-sensitive estimators, semantic deception, self-modification, real equal-budget tasks, runtime-to-formal refinement, independent reproduction, and a paper.

## Claim boundary

A PASS establishes the declared finite mechanisms and the IAIP aggregate diagnosis. It does not prove universal completeness, unbiased estimation under arbitrary source dependence, complete IAIP possession, or scientific novelty.
