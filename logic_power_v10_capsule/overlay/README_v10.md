# Logic Power v10 — Proof-Carrying Active Discovery

Logic Power v10 turns finite logical uncertainty into one of two checkable outputs:

1. an exact experiment policy that separates every pair of hypotheses with opposite answers; or
2. an exact obstruction: two opposite hypotheses indistinguishable by every admissible experiment.

## Executable scope

- finite hypotheses;
- declared deterministic experiments;
- exact rational costs and priors;
- exact minimum fixed instrumentation;
- optimal adaptive policy by worst cost and expected cost;
- conflict-guided synthesis;
- `TRUE / FALSE / UNKNOWN / INCONSISTENT / IMPOSSIBLE` monitoring;
- deterministic SHA-256 certificates;
- independent Python and Node verification;
- TLA+/TLC terminal-state checking;
- Lean boundary theorems without `sorry`.

## Scientific boundary

The finite separation theorem is established mathematics related to sufficient observations, decision trees and test covers. The contribution here is an integrated proof-carrying compiler and obstruction protocol. The internal `1000/1000` score applies only to the declared finite executable domain after every CI gate passes. It is not a claim of omniscience, universal decidability or external priority.
