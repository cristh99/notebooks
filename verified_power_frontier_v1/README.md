# Verified Power Frontier v1 — public evidence capsule

This draft branch independently reconstructs and executes the frozen finite power benchmark from private PR `cristh99/my_first_repository#98` and issue `#97`.

## Definition

**Power is verified capacity to produce, prevent, select, or orient distinct outcomes under explicit conditions, plus the capacity to expand the future set of verified capabilities.**

Technical reachability is insufficient. Unauthorized, unverified, unreliable, or insufficiently elevated high-impact policies contribute zero admissible power.

## Frozen scope

- 72 scenarios = 12 archetypes × 6 domains;
- exact rational probabilities;
- nominal, worst-case, and guaranteed goal power;
- distinct outcomes and empowerment bits;
- exact versus greedy policy frontiers;
- minimum-cost experiment separators;
- catalytic and recursive power;
- exact Shapley coalition attribution;
- SHA-256 source binding, receipts, and tamper rejection.

The source bundle is a gzip-compressed tar archive encoded as base64. CI verifies the bundle digest, decodes it, verifies every source-file digest, executes 12 unit tests and all 72 scenarios, independently verifies the receipt in Node, rejects a tampered receipt, and uploads the full evidence package.

## Score boundary

- baseline before this slice: **794/1000**;
- eligible after public CI PASS: **847/1000**;
- `1000/1000` still requires dynamic-preference correctness, continuous/open-world power, adversarial multi-agent evaluation, corrigibility under optimization pressure, real equal-budget tasks, independent reproduction, and a defensible paper.

## Claim boundary

A PASS supports the declared finite power vector and cross-domain conformance only. It does not establish universal power, safe autonomous power-seeking, correct human preferences, real-world superiority, or scientific novelty.
